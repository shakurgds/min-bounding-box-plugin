Adds min_x, min_y, max_x, max_y and extent columns, plus optional measurements: width, height, rotation angle, area, perimeter and fill ratio (how much of its bounding shape a polygon fills). Available as a dialog and as a Processing algorithm for the Toolbox, Graphical Modeler and batch mode. Works in QGIS 3.40+ and QGIS 4.
